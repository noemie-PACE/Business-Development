Executive Summary

Batch 1 focused on Priority-1 signals: brands and rights holders in Europe/North America showing fresh commercial or marketing leadership change, digital transformation spend, or women's-sport investment. Mega-scale deals (Real Madrid–Adidas, PSG–Qatar Airways, Unilever's World Cup activation) were deliberately excluded — they are already owned by Wasserman, Two Circles or in-house teams operating at a scale PACE does not compete at. The list below favours organisations where PACE's agile, execution-first model beats a large consultancy or holding-company agency on speed and fit, not on headcount.

Buying Signal Frequency (Batch 1)

What's driving the market right now

  • Europe's overall sponsorship market reached roughly €34.45bn in 2025, and women's sport is its fastest-growing segment, drawing brands like Dove, Louis Vuitton and Unilever into first-time or expanded deals. [Global Brands Magazine]
  • Women's-only sponsorship deals across Europe's top five domestic football leagues rose 53% since 2022/23, led by Serie A (+600% from a low base) and LaLiga (+79%). [SVG Europe]
  • A wave of new CMO/CCO hires in mid-2026 (Genius Sports, Formula E, BKB) are explicitly mandated to build out audience, content and digital capability rather than just brand campaigns — exactly PACE's service line, and a strong "new leader, new supplier" buying window. [Sportico]
  • Premier League clubs are actively rotating sponsorship categories away from betting toward fintech/AI-adjacent brands (e.g. Fulham–Clickhouse), creating openings for brand-side activation work with sponsors who are new to sport. [SportBusiness Sponsorship]

What "Batch 1" actually covers

500 organisations cannot be individually verified in a single research pass without either dropping citation quality or fabricating detail — both against the brief's own rules. This run scanned roughly 60 candidate organisations across sponsorship, marketing-leadership and digital-transformation signals; 9 met the evidence threshold for inclusion. The rest are logged as "scanned, not qualified" rather than silently dropped. Reaching a cumulative 500 will take several more batches — each one appends to this same dashboard's storage rather than starting over.

Known limitations, stated plainly

• No live LinkedIn access — contacts are sourced from press releases, company newsrooms and executive bios only, so several "Decision Maker" fields are intentionally blank.
• Budget figures are directional estimates based on comparable deal sizes in public reporting, not confirmed client budgets — always labelled as estimates.
• "Competitive Intensity" is scored for favourability to PACE (low intensity = high score), not raw market competitiveness.
• Weekly persistence depends on this artifact's storage — re-open the same dashboard next week and ask for a new batch to keep the history intact.
