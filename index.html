  • Premier League clubs are actively rotating sponsorship categories away from betting towards fintech/AI-adjacent brands (e.g. Fulham-Clickhouse), creating openings for brand-side activation work with sponsors who are new to sport. [SportBusiness Sponsorship]

PACE Commercial Fit

PACE's primary commercial wedge is content. The strongest opportunities have a specific, evidenced need for content production, creative services, scalable content systems, localisation, social-first content or additional production capacity. The broader PACE offering includes social and digital ecosystems, campaign activation, marketing strategy, growth amplification, paid performance and sponsorship advisory.

Leads are not scored simply because an organisation spends money on sports marketing. The strongest opportunities combine a fresh buying signal with a specific problem PACE can solve, a realistic CHF 20,000-100,000 workstream and a credible route to the decision-maker.

Particular weight is given to situations where a new sponsorship, event, campaign, market expansion or digital initiative creates additional content and creative requirements that an existing team may struggle to deliver without additional capacity.

What "Batch 1" actually covers

500 organisations cannot be individually verified in a single research pass without either dropping citation quality or fabricating detail, both against the brief's own rules. This run scanned roughly 60 candidate organisations across sponsorship, marketing-leadership and digital-transformation signals; 9 met the evidence threshold for inclusion. The rest are logged as "scanned, not qualified" rather than silently dropped. Reaching a cumulative 500 will take several more batches, each one appends to this same dashboard's storage rather than starting over.

Known limitations, stated plainly

• No live LinkedIn access, contacts are sourced from press releases, company newsrooms and executive bios only, so several "Decision Maker" fields are intentionally blank.
• Direct executive emails are essentially never published in the sources this brief permits (LinkedIn, company site, press release, bio, annual report). Press releases only list a company PR/IR contact, not the named executive's own address. Rather than infer a likely address from a company's email pattern, every contact's email is left blank unless independently confirmed, the one exception this batch (Genius Sports) is labelled as a company press contact, not the CMO's personal email.
• Budget figures are directional estimates based on comparable deal sizes in public reporting, not confirmed client budgets, always labelled as estimates.
• "Competitive Intensity" is scored for favourability to PACE (low intensity = high score), not raw market competitiveness.
• Weekly persistence depends on this artefact's storage: re-open the same dashboard next week and ask for a new batch to keep the history intact.

Scope recalibration (from batch 3 onward)

Two rules apply to all batches: (1) Scope is Europe and South Africa only, with budgets scoped to CHF 20,000-100,000. MENA was removed from scope from batch 9 onward; the six MENA-market leads researched under the earlier Europe/MENA/South Africa scope (Ellington Properties, Al-Qadsiah FC, Al Wasl Club, FRMF, Lusail Sports Club, Al Wahda FC) were moved out of focus rather than deleted, and remain visible only as historical, out-of-scope records. (2) Team-size realism: PACE is a ~30-person team, so organisations whose scale would put PACE up against holding-company agencies on a like-for-like service breadth (e.g. On, ICC Cricket World Cup 2027, MotoGP SEG) are flagged with a "scale caution" rather than excluded outright, since the underlying signal can still be genuine even where the realistic engagement is a narrow, specialist slice rather than the full mandate.
