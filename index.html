Executive Summary

Batch 1 focused on Priority-1 signals: brands and rights holders showing fresh commercial or marketing leadership change, digital transformation spend, or women's-sport investment. Mega-scale deals (Real Madrid–Adidas, PSG–Qatar Airways, Unilever's World Cup activation) were deliberately excluded — they are already owned by Wasserman, Two Circles or in-house teams operating at a scale PACE does not compete at. From batch 5 onward, scope was tightened further to Europe/MENA/South Africa only, CHF 20,000–100,000 budgets. The list below favours organisations where PACE's agile, execution-first model beats a large consultancy or holding-company agency on speed and fit, not on headcount.

Assessment

Of the confirmed 2027 events found this batch, none currently offer a live 1YTG window under the corrected rule (event must be more than 12 months out, i.e. land after roughly August 2027, to leave PACE time to pitch and plan before the anniversary) — Crans-Montana, Lausanne and the Swiss Aquatics championships are all already inside 12 months out, meaning the window to sell in a 1YTG campaign for them has closed. EuroBeachVolley Gstaad 2027's exact dates weren't confirmed in this search — if it lands in autumn 2027 rather than summer, it would clear the 12-month threshold and be worth pitching now; this needs a direct check with Sports Events Gstaad before treating it as live. The Switzerland 2038 Olympic bid is the strongest genuine long-lead opportunity found — bid committees typically start building public narrative years before a hosting vote, so an early relationship (logged in Opportunities) is more valuable than waiting for a 1YTG moment that's over a decade away.

What's driving the market right now

  • Europe's overall sponsorship market reached roughly €34.45bn in 2025, and women's sport is its fastest-growing segment, drawing brands like Dove, Louis Vuitton and Unilever into first-time or expanded deals. [Global Brands Magazine]
  • Women's-only sponsorship deals across Europe's top five domestic football leagues rose 53% since 2022/23, led by Serie A (+600% from a low base) and LaLiga (+79%). [SVG Europe]
  • A wave of new CMO/CCO hires in mid-2026 (Genius Sports, Formula E, BKB) are explicitly mandated to build out audience, content and digital capability rather than just brand campaigns — exactly PACE's service line, and a strong "new leader, new supplier" buying window. [Sportico]
  • Premier League clubs are actively rotating sponsorship categories away from betting toward fintech/AI-adjacent brands (e.g. Fulham–Clickhouse), creating openings for brand-side activation work with sponsors who are new to sport. [SportBusiness Sponsorship]

What "Batch 1" actually covers

500 organisations cannot be individually verified in a single research pass without either dropping citation quality or fabricating detail — both against the brief's own rules. This run scanned roughly 60 candidate organisations across sponsorship, marketing-leadership and digital-transformation signals; 9 met the evidence threshold for inclusion. The rest are logged as "scanned, not qualified" rather than silently dropped. Reaching a cumulative 500 will take several more batches — each one appends to this same dashboard's storage rather than starting over.

Known limitations, stated plainly

• No live LinkedIn access — contacts are sourced from press releases, company newsrooms and executive bios only, so several "Decision Maker" fields are intentionally blank.
• Direct executive emails are essentially never published in the sources this brief permits (LinkedIn, company site, press release, bio, annual report). Press releases only list a company PR/IR contact, not the named executive's own address. Rather than infer a likely address from a company's email pattern, every contact's email is left blank unless independently confirmed — the one exception this batch (Genius Sports) is labelled as a company press contact, not the CMO's personal email.
• Budget figures are directional estimates based on comparable deal sizes in public reporting, not confirmed client budgets — always labelled as estimates.
• "Competitive Intensity" is scored for favourability to PACE (low intensity = high score), not raw market competitiveness.
• Weekly persistence depends on this artifact's storage — re-open the same dashboard next week and ask for a new batch to keep the history intact.

Scope recalibration (from batch 3 onward)

Two changes now apply to all future batches: (1) North America has been removed entirely as a scanning region — it is not tracked, not scored, and prior North American entries have been purged from the database rather than merely deprioritised. Scope is now Europe, MENA and South Africa only, with budgets scoped to CHF 20,000–100,000. (2) Team-size realism — PACE is a ~30-person team, so organisations whose scale would put PACE up against holding-company agencies on a like-for-like service breadth (e.g. On, ICC Cricket World Cup 2027, MotoGP SEG) are flagged with a "scale caution" rather than excluded outright, since the underlying signal can still be genuine even where the realistic engagement is a narrow, specialist slice rather than the full mandate.
